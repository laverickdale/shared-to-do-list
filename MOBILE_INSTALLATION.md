# 📱 How to Install LavTask on Your Phone

This guide will help you install the **LavTask** app on your Android or iOS phone in simple steps. No coding knowledge needed!

---

## ✅ What You Need

- A smartphone (iPhone or Android)
- Internet connection (WiFi or mobile data)
- A few minutes of your time

---

## 🤖 For Android Users

### Step 1: Open a Web Browser
1. Pick any browser on your phone (Chrome, Firefox, Samsung Internet, etc.)
2. Tap the address bar at the top

### Step 2: Go to the App Website
1. Type or paste this address: https://shared-to-do-list-psi.vercel.app/
2. Press "Go" or tap the search button
3. Wait for the page to load completely

### Step 3: Install the App
1. Look for a **"+" icon** or **"⋮" menu** (three dots) in the top right corner of your browser
2. Tap it and look for one of these options:
   - **"Install app"**
   - **"Add to Home screen"**
   - **"Create shortcut"**
3. Tap the option you see
4. A popup will appear — tap **"Install"** or **"Add"**

### Step 4: Find Your App
1. Look at your home screen or app drawer (usually at the bottom of your screen)
2. Find the icon labeled **"LavTask"** or **"Tasks"**
3. Tap it to open the app!

### Step 5: (Optional) Pin to Home Screen
1. To make it easier to find, hold down the app icon
2. Select **"Add to Home screen"**
3. The app icon will now appear on your main home screen

---

## 🍎 For iPhone Users

### Step 1: Open Safari Browser
1. Tap the **Safari** app (the compass icon)
   - If you don't see it, swipe left on your home screen and search for "Safari"

### Step 2: Go to the App Website
1. Tap the address bar at the bottom of the screen
2. Type or paste this address: https://shared-to-do-list-psi.vercel.app/
3. Press "Go"
4. Wait for the page to load completely

### Step 3: Install the App
1. Tap the **Share button** (the box with an arrow pointing upward) at the bottom
2. Scroll right through the options until you find **"Add to Home Screen"**
   - Tip: It might not be immediately visible — keep scrolling!
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**

### Step 4: Name Your App (Optional)
1. You'll see the app name in a text field — you can keep it or change it
2. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner

### Step 5: Find Your App
1. Go back to your home screen
2. Look for the icon labeled **"LavTask"** or **"Tasks"**
3. Tap it to open the app!

## 🎯 Using Your New App

Once you've installed the app:

1. **Create tasks** — Tap the **+** button to add a new task
2. **Share with others** — Use the **Shared sync setup** to connect with other users
3. **Voice entry** — On mobile, tap **Voice task** to say your tasks out loud
4. **Check boxes** — Tap completed tasks to mark them done

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "I don't see an Install button"
- Make sure you're using a compatible browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
- Try refreshing the page (pull down on the page)
- Check that you have a good internet connection

### "The app won't open"
- Close the browser tab and try opening the app icon again
- Restart your phone
- Delete the app shortcut and reinstall it

### "Voice task doesn't work"
- Make sure you've allowed microphone access when the app asked
- Go to Settings → Apps → LavTasks → Permissions → Microphone (Android)
- Go to Settings → Safari → Microphone (iOS)

### "I want to sync with others"
- Tap **"Shared sync setup"** in the app
- Ask your admin for the Supabase URL and key
- Enter them in the app to connect
